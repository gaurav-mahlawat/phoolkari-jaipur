A little story about us

Clothes should feel like a good day.

Phoolkari began with a simple thought: the things we wear every day can carry a little more joy. We are a small Jaipur based label making easy, thoughtful clothing with colour, craft and comfort at heart.

We work closely with local makers and artisans, celebrating the beauty of hand-block printing and the human hands behind every piece. Our collections are made in small batches, with care at each step.

We hope you find something here that feels like you. Made with love, always.
